# Persuasive Essay Rubric

**English 101 — Essay Writing · Grade 10 · Total: 50 points**

---

## Criteria

- **Thesis clarity and focus — 10 points**
  The essay presents a specific, debatable thesis at the end of the introduction
  and stays focused on it throughout. Full marks: the thesis is precise and every
  paragraph advances it.

- **Quality of supporting evidence — 15 points**
  The argument is supported by relevant, credible evidence (sources, statistics,
  expert quotations) cited in MLA format. Full marks: at least three strong
  sources, each introduced, cited, and explained (ICE pattern).

- **Organization and structure — 10 points**
  The essay follows a logical structure: engaging hook, clear introduction,
  ordered body paragraphs, and a conclusion that synthesizes and ends with a call
  to action. Full marks: transitions connect every paragraph.

- **Use of rhetorical appeals — 10 points**
  The essay deliberately balances ethos, pathos, and logos. Full marks: appeals
  are used appropriately, and a counterargument is conceded and effectively
  refuted.

- **Grammar and mechanics — 5 points**
  Sentence structure, spelling, punctuation, and formatting are correct and
  consistent. Full marks: no errors that distract the reader.

---

**Total possible points: 50**

**Scoring bands:** 45–50 Excellent · 35–44 Proficient · 25–34 Developing ·
Below 25 Needs revision
