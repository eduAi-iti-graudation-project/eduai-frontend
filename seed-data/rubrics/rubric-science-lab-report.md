# Science Lab Report Rubric

**Science 301 — Lab Reports · Grade 10 · Total: 45 points**

---

## Criteria

- **Hypothesis and background — 5 points**
  The report gives accurate scientific background on enzymes and states a clear,
  testable hypothesis. Full marks: background is relevant and the hypothesis
  predicts a specific relationship between temperature and reaction rate.

- **Procedure and variables — 10 points**
  Materials and procedure are described clearly enough to be repeated, and the
  independent, dependent, and controlled variables are correctly identified.
  Full marks: all variables are named and the procedure controls for them.

- **Data collection and results — 10 points**
  Data table is complete (three trials per temperature), units are correct, and
  results are summarized accurately, including the bar graph of mean foam height
  vs. temperature. Full marks: all trials recorded, means calculated, graph
  labeled correctly.

- **Analysis and conclusion — 15 points**
  The analysis answers the questions with scientific reasoning (optimum
  temperature, denaturation), and the conclusion evaluates the hypothesis and
  applies the findings to a real-world context. Full marks: explanations are
  precise and evidence-based.

- **Formatting and clarity — 5 points**
  The report follows the required section structure, is well organized, and uses
  correct spelling and grammar. Full marks: all six sections present, neat and
  professional presentation.

---

**Total possible points: 45**

**Scoring bands:** 40–45 Excellent · 32–39 Proficient · 22–31 Developing ·
Below 22 Needs revision
