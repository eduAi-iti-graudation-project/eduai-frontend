# Genetics Problem Set Rubric

**Science 301 — Lab Reports · Grade 10 · Total: 30 points**

---

## Criteria

- **Correct Punnett squares — 10 points**
  Each Punnett square is set up correctly with alleles defined, parental
  genotypes written, and gametes placed correctly. Full marks: all seven
  square-based problems show complete, accurate squares.

- **Inheritance ratios — 10 points**
  Genotypic and phenotypic ratios (or percentages) are correctly derived from
  the squares, including incomplete dominance, codominance, and sex-linked
  results. Full marks: every ratio and probability is correct.

- **Terminology and explanations — 5 points**
  Terminology (dominant, recessive, homozygous, heterozygous, carrier, allele,
  genotype, phenotype) is used correctly, and written explanations justify the
  answers. Full marks: clear, concise reasoning in every problem.

- **Pedigree interpretation — 5 points**
  The pedigree is correctly classified as dominant or recessive and autosomal or
  X-linked, with a justified two-to-three-sentence explanation. Full marks: the
  reasoning references the key pedigree clues (skips generations, affects both
  sexes, unaffected parents with an affected child).
