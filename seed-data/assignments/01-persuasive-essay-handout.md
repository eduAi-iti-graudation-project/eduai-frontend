# Persuasive Essay Assignment — Prompt and Requirements

**English 101 — Essay Writing · Grade 10 · Due Friday, September 4, 2026**

---

## Prompt

**"Artificial intelligence is transforming the way students learn. Write a
persuasive essay arguing for OR against the use of AI tools (such as ChatGPT or
AI study assistants) in high school classrooms. Take a clear position and defend
it with reasoning and evidence."**

## Task

Write a persuasive essay of **600–800 words** (approximately 3–4 pages,
double-spaced) that:

1. States a clear, debatable thesis in the introduction.
2. Develops the argument in **at least three body paragraphs** using the ICE
   pattern (Introduce, Cite, Explain).
3. Addresses **one counterargument** and refutes it.
4. Ends with a conclusion that restates the thesis and ends with a call to
   action.

## Requirements

- **Length:** 600–800 words (word count noted at the end).
- **Sources:** cite at least **three sources** (articles, studies, or expert
  quotations). Use MLA format for in-text citations.
- **Rhetorical appeals:** consciously use a balance of **ethos, pathos, and
  logos**.
- **Transitions:** use transition phrases to connect paragraphs.
- **Format:** double-spaced, 12 pt Times New Roman or Calibri, 1-inch margins.

## Suggested Outline

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Introduction | Hook (statistic, question, or anecdote) + thesis |
| Body 1 | Strongest argument with cited evidence |
| Body 2 | Second argument with cited evidence |
| Body 3 | Counterargument → concession → rebuttal |
| Conclusion | Synthesis + call to action |
