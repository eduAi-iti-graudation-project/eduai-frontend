## Instructions

Solve all eight problems. For each Punnett-square problem you must:

1. Define the alleles (e.g., R = round, r = wrinkled).
2. Write the genotypes of the parents.
3. Draw the complete Punnett square.
4. State the **genotypic ratio** and the **phenotypic ratio** (or percentages)

## Problems

### 1. Monohybrid cross — pea seed shape (5 points)

In pea plants, round seeds (R) are dominant over wrinkled seeds (r). A
homozygous round-seeded plant is crossed with a wrinkled-seeded plant.
Determine the genotypes and phenotypes of the F₁ offspring.

### 2. Heterozygote cross — pod color (5 points)

Green pods (G) are dominant over yellow pods (g). Two heterozygous plants are
crossed. Give the genotypic and phenotypic ratios of the offspring.

### 3. Test cross (5 points)

A tall plant (T = tall, t = short) has an unknown genotype. When crossed with a
short plant, half the offspring are tall and half are short. What was the
genotype of the tall parent? Explain your reasoning.

### 4. Incomplete dominance — snapdragons (5 points)

In snapdragons, flower color shows incomplete dominance: RR = red, rr = white,
Rr = pink. A pink plant is crossed with a white plant. Give the phenotypic ratio
of the offspring.

### 5. Codominance — ABO blood types (5 points)

A mother has type AB blood (IᵃIᵇ) and a father has type O blood (ii). What
blood types are possible in their children, and in what percentages?

### 6. Sex-linked trait — color blindness (5 points)

Color blindness is an X-linked recessive trait (Xᶜ). A carrier mother (XˣXᶜ)
marries a normal-vision father (XY). What is the probability that a son will be
color blind? What is the probability that a daughter will be a carrier?

### 7. Dihybrid cross (5 points)

In garden peas, round seeds (R) are dominant over wrinkled (r), and yellow seeds
(Y) are dominant over green (y). A plant with genotype RrYy is self-fertilized.
Give the expected phenotypic ratio of the offspring.

### 8. Pedigree interpretation (5 points)

A pedigree shows a rare genetic disorder. The trait appears in both sexes, skips
a generation, and two unaffected parents produce an affected child. Determine
whether the trait is most likely dominant or recessive, and state whether it is
autosomal or X-linked. Justify your answer in two or three sentences.
